Where is my information saved?

Your Mac user folder contains .outlook_assistant_config.json (settings), .outlook_assistant_tasks.json (tasks), .outlook_assistant_task_review.json (review history), and .outlook_assistant_chat.json (chat). Reply drafts are saved in .outlook_assistant_drafts.json; the browser also keeps a recovery copy. Automatic recovery copies are in .outlook_assistant_backups. A laptop restart does not erase these files.
